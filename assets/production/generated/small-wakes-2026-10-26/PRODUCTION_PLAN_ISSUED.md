## Candle Snail — 1--

One low, broad cemetery snail with a dark moss-teal body and a charcoal wax spiral shell carrying one thick ivory candle. Two short eye stalks; no human face. Quiet bone/teal/ochre masses, cracked wax with a few broad drips. Grounded foot, no hops or flight. Four idle/foot-wave poses, four deliberate candle-tilt release poses, four shell-slump defeats; complete transparent source returns preserved.

Signature: Waxwake. Slow collision-resolved glide leaves a locked warned wax rail, then a short active burn. The second visible half-health phase, Twin Wick, places two separated parallel rails with a broad quiet center and faster lateral gliding between casts. Cross a rail before it burns or use the central opening. It never obscures the playfield. Brutal adds a delayed cross-cut with its own warning and locked origin, retaining an escape opening, plus real doubled projectile volleys. Normal is an entry encounter with two phases and no blanket health increase. Original score: Slow Wick, G minor,9/8,88 BPM; sparse plucked/droplet phrase and bowed pedal.

## Cobble Crab — 1-

A compact undead street crab assembled from broad grey cobbles, one rusty square drain grate on its back, six jointed stone walking legs and two visibly unequal stone claws. One chipped claw and one broad intact claw are identity locks. No frog/shell-snail silhouette and no humanoid head. Four true scuttle poses, four opening/clamping poses, four rubble-collapse defeats.

Signature: Grate Clamp. A marked pincer pair closes toward a locked strip after a long tell. The crab scuttles sideways between clamps. The second visible phase, Cross Streets, alternates the clamp axis and adds a second delayed closure while leaving the marked outer lanes usable. Counterplay is to leave the locked strip and pass through the opening after a clamp; a finite loose anchor stone can cancel its linked closure. Brutal creates a second warned anchor/closure and extra locked volleys, under existing caps. Original score: Stones Remember, D minor,5/4,104 BPM; dry struck-stone pattern and low reed response.

## Mourning Kettle — 1

A squat blackened funeral kettle, copper seam, one long bent spout on viewer-right, one fixed iron hoop handle on viewer-left, one ivory mourning ribbon tied under the lid, two short broad iron feet, no hands or human face. A single red pressure valve is an identity mark. Four cautious waddle/idle poses, four lid-pressure/pour poses, four tilted cold-collapse defeats.

Signature: Pressure Pour. A locked steam lane heats after a visible warning; condensation beads cross it only after the first pour subsides. The second half-health phase, Last Service, alternates two separated spout lanes with staggered bead timing. Step sideways during the tell, cross after steam falls, or break a finite remote valve to cancel its linked pour. Brutal adds a delayed rear pour and extra warned bead volley while retaining a cold corridor. Original score: Tea for the Absent, F-sharp minor,6/8,92 BPM; breathy reed/soft metal and descending minor phrase.
