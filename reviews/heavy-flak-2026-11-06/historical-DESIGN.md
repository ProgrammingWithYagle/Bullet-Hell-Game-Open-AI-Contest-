# Heavy pathway audit — working material

No Heavy specialization is registered by this file. Continue and verify the current Soldier slice before changing the playable catalog. This is a design audit, not another class declared complete, a fixed three-route roster, final balance or human acceptance.

Hux Bell's existing Heavy is sustained fire and suppression:140health/190speed; Suppressing Cone fires fourteen real slowing rounds, Braced Firing grants its issued protection/fire-rate with slower movement, and Foundry Overture supplies temporary unlimited primary ammunition/pierce/fire-rate with reduced movement. General already owns Split Suppression's two fans, Shoulder Mantlet's following physical wall, Furnace Vents' altered overdrive and Reserve Plating's difficulty-limited shield cap. Preserve all these IDs, costs/effects and ownership. Do not sell them again under new names.

Candidate families remain open. A Siege Gunner would commit a deliberate sector and operate a finite timed firing sequence; an Interdictor would contest an actual incoming lane rather than acquire passive projectile immunity; an Armored Anchor would choose which direction and ground can be defended, with actual difficulty/cover and body rules. More fitting paths can emerge. Names and node counts do not justify paid entries.

The first candidate worth prototyping is a **controlled traverse**, distinct from Soldier's settled single round or instant cover bank and from the issued instant cone. It would emit a finite series over real combat ticks across an explicitly chosen manual sector. The player must decide when to begin, which side/arc to sweep, whether to stop, where to put the actual vulnerable body, and how to redirect only the remaining work. It cannot hit through bodies/cover, teleport its muzzle, choose a target, bank future missed emissions or spend ammunition twice.

Proposed early structure, not final values: an affordable2SP entry after the existing1SP Ordnance foundation, reachable with Short3SP. One C starts the real series; a later C cuts it. Each accepted emission spends one actual magazine round. Start rejection for busy/empty/capacity spends no recovery or ammunition. Recovery begins only with the first actual emission. A later blocked emission does not backfill a burst when capacity returns; explicit stopping/expiry loses the remaining opportunity, with no refund of already emitted rounds. Other actions and owner/weapon loss have explicit cancellation rules. There is no collected heat/scrap/pressure currency.

An early geometry fork could trade broad sector coverage for a narrow rake while retaining the same real shot count/time/ammunition. It must change actual trajectories and target exposure rather than merely display a damage bonus. An acquired B could halt the current traverse at its actual barrel bearing for the remaining scheduled rounds, without adding rounds, retiming the next emission or returning recovery. A later exclusive handling choice could permit slow actual carriage movement or a single bounded cease-fire within the same original sequence deadline. These remain hypotheses until a prototype shows that the operations are useful and readable.

The central unresolved design question is value: ordinary primary fire and the issued Q/E already perform useful sustained firing. A traverse that only automates weaker primary shots would not earn a paid entry. Its authored physical sector, actual suppression/contact response, sustained output and vulnerability must produce a real alternative without making Q/E obsolete. Do not register an empty card while this is unresolved. A high-grade culmination must qualitatively change how the sequence is operated; avoid copying every Soldier culmination into another during-recovery follow-up. No capstone is chosen yet.

Interdictor remains more expensive engineering: genuine projectile interception needs consistent within-tick trajectories for both shots. Never infer a hostile path by subtracting velocity from its final position when it may have bounced, curved or changed speed. Beams/fields/ground effects are not automatically shootable bullets. Prototype exact moving-circle ordering, cover/body precedence, ownership, finite limits and shared cue privacy before considering admission. Existing Tank/Swordsman counterplay must remain distinct.

Preserve27SP/onecapstone, all twelve class labels/equipment/balances, remaps/both HUD choices, old Practice/history editions and owner-only friendly streams. The next content admission must use the actually published Soldier edition, not a workspace prototype revision. New operations need meaningful authority tests, ordinary earned-budget MenuDOM/whole-worker/real-peer proof, natural/safe-replay comparisons and current exact-source CI/frozen review. Human/rendered/device/native-language/listening/motion/final balance remains open. Individual bosses, armory and Campaign remain later.
