# Heavy and Assassin directional actions

Development build `2026.10.24-heavy-assassin-actions`, protocol 13. Heavy and Assassin each gain twenty clips and 88 new poses: idle, primary firearm attack, hurt, dodge and downed in four independent directions. Each keeps 32 existing run poses, giving 120 per class. Five classes now have all six action families drawn; Soldier and Medic still need action libraries and independent side runs. Selected production motion totals 1,143 poses. Rendered transitions and human/device acceptance remain open.

Heavy retains his beard, mustard coat, physical left-shoulder to right-waist bandolier and single rotary firearm held by the right trigger hand and left forward cradle hand. Assassin retains his black hair, red face scarf, torn rust cape and single brass carbine held right at the trigger and left at the fore-end. Matte ink/gouache sheets avoid baked-in projectiles and effects. Native sheets and 56/112-pixel contacts were inspected as assistant art candidates.

All twenty Heavy returns and all twenty-three Assassin returns, exact requests, facing references and portable input hashes are preserved in `docs/heavy-actions-2026-10-24.json` and `docs/assassin-actions-2026-10-24.json`. Three Assassin v1 sheets had touching figures that could not pass whole-pose extraction. Complete replacements for north/west attacks and west dodge remain selected; all v1 attempts are retained. No direction is mirrored and no limb is cut, repainted or repositioned.

Technical packing uses one upright-calibrated scale for each complete strip. Resting and crouched poses retain their natural lower height. Where transparent native extents need more room, a complete clip receives symmetrically expanded cells and an equivalent origin/display scale. The character body size stays constant; faint source pixels are preserved. Whole-figure ownership masks are allowed only when complete figures are disconnected and every nonzero-alpha source pixel is conserved. Independent verification reconstructs every atlas pixel, checks connected figures, references and transparent padding. Failed strict-gutter/component and early padding attempts remain documented under `reviews/heavy-actions-2026-10-24/` and `reviews/assassin-actions-2026-10-24/`.

The existing presentation clock selects these clips from authoritative ammo, health, dodge and player status. Real primary shots exercise all four attack facings for both classes; reduced motion uses stable representative poses. The full pipeline includes all prior interruption, fall hold, revive, late-observer and participant/class/run isolation checks. Game rules and protocol are unchanged.
