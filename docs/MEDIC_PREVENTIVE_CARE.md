# Medic Preventive Care

Buy the Chemistry foundation for 1 SP and Precaution Wrap for 2 SP. With both hands free, stop and use Action 3. Aim at a living ally within 88 pixels along a clear line, or aim away from the team to prepare yourself. The patient can start at full health. The wrap lasts 2.2 seconds. Healing Field stays available during an unused wrap and casting it ends that reserve.

A nonlethal hostile projectile, an accepted charge contact or a Stitchwright seam can spend the original Healing Field recovery. The first qualifying impact schedules one small dose five combat intervals later. Armor, shields and damage take effect first. The dose uses the ordinary healing modifiers, with a ceiling of 14 base HP and 48% of the accepted health loss. An unused wrap has a short retry delay. A qualifying hit pays the recovery even if a down or disconnect interrupts the delayed dose. Periodic status effects, fixed environmental damage and ordinary revival have their usual rules. Field Dressing uses the same Q recovery. Rapid Response uses E.

**Doorstep Kit.** Wrap yourself, then use Action 3 on valid ground to set it down once. A 23-pixel ring marks the station. The first eligible player to walk into it from outside takes the same reserve until the original deadline. Ordinary voluntary movement supplies the crossing. Clear ground and the original Healing Field availability still apply.

**Layered Wrap.** Use Action 3 again in reach before the first hit to layer the same patient's wrap. It records up to two qualifying wounds. The first spends Q. After one or two wounds, stand within reach and use Action 3 before expiry to give one bounded dose on the original recovery. Both fittings cost 2 SP at five earned SP. The first Short clear provides the actual 9-SP shop. The fittings exclude each other.

The owner sees the remapped action, availability and captured hits. The physical wrap, station and delivered dose appear in the room. Both HUD layouts preserve the saved control mappings. The three emblems retain their original image bytes. Seven short operation cues accompany the private action state.
