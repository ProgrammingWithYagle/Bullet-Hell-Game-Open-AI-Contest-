# The first return: playable opening

Two longer main-story scenes now replace the short prologue and first report in English, Spanish and Arabic. They use the existing visual-novel reader, painted Gatehouse and survivor portraits. The English scenes have3,027words. `tools/story-word-count.mjs` records5,007authored playable English main-story words including the other unique base passages, route decisions and ending alternatives; duplicate readings, optional letters, translations, guide copy and unimplemented drafts are excluded. This is not a one-playthrough count or a writing-quality score. The five-million-word target remains far ahead.

`src/client/story-opening.ts` owns stable scene bindings and validates prose-only editions. Narrative packages cannot carry maps, rewards or route commands. The actual opening is the existing shared Constable/Gatehouse encounter. All seven classes can replay it in Story or supported customized Free Play, retaining practice exclusions. Narrative return obligations and custody are recorded in `story-continuity/OPENING.md`.

During an untimed Story aftermath, `ui/story-encounter.ts` shows the actual defeated boss's fixed classification/form and the local survivor's role-normalized equipped-build grade through shared helpers. It appears only at this known-clear boundary, preserving concealed future contracts. Reading still never presses Ready, changes a vote or grants rewards. Three editions have independent bookmarks, and chapter entry rechecks earned eligibility. Current pages preserve paragraph breaks without inventing speech attribution for third-person prose.

The manuscript has been revised and integrated, but human/native-language/literary/pacing/device review remains open. The full multi-volume engine, optional personal chapters, larger arcs, wider side-cast development and completed boss arcs remain unfinished. Static loading of these small editions is retained; lazy volume delivery remains future work.

The candidate now opens a full-viewport native modal screen for chapters and earned reports. Native controller checks cover keyboard navigation, Escape, return focus, disposal and preservation across UI rebinding. Story aftermaths auto-open once, preserving an explicit close. Browser automation remains disabled, and rendered sizing still needs human review.

Story mutation votes are removed: after party Ready, the next phase is the concealed route choice. Free Play retains its mutation flow. Recognized older local Story saves clear obsolete mutation bonuses; an old mutation-vote boundary returns to untimed reading without inheriting Ready. New/unknown/authority snapshots cannot use that local conversion to smuggle bonuses. The later cart passage now says next cart in all three editions.

The requested mastery progression replacing SP, slower resource economy, nonboss encounters, shared-class wider cast, human antagonists, emotion sprites and character voices are recorded in STORY_CAMPAIGN_REDESIGN.md and VISUAL_NOVEL_DIRECTION.md. They remain unfinished. Current Story still has the prototype SP curve and fifteen boss encounters; these have not been disguised as the proposed new progression.
