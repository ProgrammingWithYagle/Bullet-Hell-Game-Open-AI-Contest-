# Assassin Duelist

Buy Fine Needle for 1 SP and Called Opening for 2 SP. Stand still and aim at one visible enemy 82 to 222 pixels away. Press Action 1 to mark it for about 2.17 seconds. Walk across either shoulder until the opening appears, then stop, aim at that enemy and press again to spend Shadow Step recovery on one strike. Walking 52 pixels across its bearing makes the opening. Walk back across that bearing and you lose the ground you measured. The focus clears if you lose sight, move out of reach, dodge, sprint, use Shadow Step or invoke Unwritten Passage.

- **Passing Cut.** When the opening is ready, continue along the measured side and press Action 1 in that stride. The strike starts from the step you actually took.
- **Counterturn.** When the opening is ready, turn across the enemy’s bearing and press Action 1 in that real reverse step. Measure 34 pixels on the return, then stop and strike before the original focus ends.

A fitting costs 2 SP, requires five earned points and excludes the other fitting. The two fittings share the same strike and recovery. Directional armor, weak-point contact and cover still govern the hit. The Assassin sees the live movement prompt and hears seven action cues. Teammates see the marked enemy and actual strike.
