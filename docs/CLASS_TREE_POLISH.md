## Sentry aiming correction

The old carriage follow goal rotated with player aim. In an isolated six-second fixture, fixed aim fired 14 shots while sweeping aim fired zero: movement repeatedly restarted the brace timer.

A carriage now retains its deployed offset from its owner and follows their translation. Its barrel still aims at targets independently. Turning aim alone leaves a stationary machine braced and able to fire. Moving the owner still relocates the carriage at the existing 150 pixels/second, stops fire and requires the existing half-second brace afterward. Crossfire Pair keeps distinct offsets; ordinary terrain/cover rejection, finite life, owner cleanup, turret bonuses and siege overclock remain.

This is a correction to the existing technique. It adds no new active slot, resource, grade, SP budget or damage bonus.
