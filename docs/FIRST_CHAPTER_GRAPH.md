# First expanded chapter: One Place Left

**Current direction, 2026-11-01:** Story Campaign exclusively uses the new chapter/learning system. Free Play preserves the original story-free SP runs. Do not add a second fast SP-based Story start. Earlier Story prototype saves/archives remain compatibility data, not a fresh Campaign mode. See [OPENING_CHAPTER.md](OPENING_CHAPTER.md) for the first playable implementation and its limits.

This broader blueprint extends the current three-objective introductory chapter. Its richer investigation, defense, escort and consequence nodes remain future work; OPENING_CHAPTER.md records the actual simplified runtime graph. It reuses the published gate opening, Constable encounter and first return, then gives them a slower continuation. Main-story completion still supports any class and co-op.

The dramatic question is practical: can the party establish a route that people can keep using? Opening a gate is not the same as maintaining water, shelter, transport and trust. The chapter gives the cast time to work and disagree before its second boss. It does not reveal the full bell mechanism or resolve Lorn's entire evacuation.

## A wider class community

Kesa Morn is a proposed established Engineer who maintains the ward's equipment. Kesa wants repairs the local crews can maintain and can oppose Tamsin's clever temporary solution. Drin Hal is a proposed Medic practitioner who knows which patients depend on equipment that cannot travel. Drin can disagree with Iona without being a villain or an infallible teacher. Neither has a finalized grade or delivered portrait yet.

Miri Fen remains a copyist with their own obligations and past compromises. Senn remains a porter; that profession does not automatically assign a combat class. Evi and Jorren should recur as people doing things, not an indefinitely grateful rescue tableau. New cast IDs must be independent of class IDs.

A proposed living antagonist, Vey Rusk, controls transport allocations and access to the workshop route. Their leverage comes from people and material power, not a hidden transformation into another undead boss. Give Vey a concrete decision, beneficiaries, relationships and consequences before writing a confrontation. A position in an institution does not make every colleague complicit in the same way. These three new people are working proposals, not established playable scenes or completed arcs.

## Training tied to the actual techniques

The first proof candidates use the existing D- support techniques: soldier-command-1, heavy-suppression-1, tank-protection-1, medic-triage-1, mage-control-1, engineer-constructs-1 and assassin-ambush-1. Their actual effects and limits come from the shared catalog. This list is a binding reference, not a declaration that the new mastery implementation already unlocks them.

The chapter should show learning, relevant practice and proof. A solo player must have a valid route without needing a teammate to be deliberately injured. Cooperative alternatives must recognize support, protection and control. Optional personal episodes can later offer alternate proofs, but must not become mandatory to finish the main story.

Horizontal choices and practical competence can grow while the class remains in the early bands. Do not make a new ability impressive by ignoring costs, cooldowns, reach or counterplay. Do not attach SS+ to an early training reward merely because the prototype can purchase a depth6node. Long-term grading still needs the implemented mastery record and wider story structure.

## First shared regular encounter

Build ward-approach as an actual ordinary-enemy objective. Start with the existing humanoid ordinary-actor art/movement foundation and identify any reused production art honestly. Do not claim a completed zombie/skeleton/wraith roster. Add distinct archetypes and art through later production.

The objective owns bounded waves, announced legal spawn points, remaining-enemy counts, navigation, defeat/timeout and cleanup. No active boss body or boss-clear reward should drive its completion. Existing ordinary-actor projectile, area damage, slow, decoy and navigation paths should remain shared. Provide a supported Free Play replay and preserve customized/practice record exclusions.

Story integration needs chapter/node receipts and eligibility separate from the old fifteen-boss campaign record. A regular clear may advance a mission but cannot manufacture a Constable kill or boss stamp. Midchapter arrival cannot inherit earlier proof or chapter completion. Cooperative reading and choices need explicit Ready/choice boundaries beyond post-boss intermissions.
