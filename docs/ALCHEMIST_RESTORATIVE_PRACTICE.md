# Alchemist Restorative Practice

Buy Roll the Sleeves and Set Dressing with three earned Free Play skill points. Prepare Mending and wait for Cast Flask to be ready. Aim at nearby ground and use Action 3. The owner sees where the real flask will land. It spends one prepared Mending and its ordinary throw recovery. A new obstruction can stop the flight.

On landing, the flask leaves one dressing for seven seconds. A living injured survivor who stays inside the clear circle for seven tenths of a second can use it. An injured Alchemist can use their own dressing in Solo. Leaving the circle or losing a clear line restarts the wait. Surplus healing ends with the portion. One placement can remain in flight or on the ground at a time.

Two fittings become available at five earned points.

- **Shared Roll** divides each dressing into two half-strength portions. Two different injured survivors can use one each.
- **Quick Knot** puts a single portion in a smaller circle for the Alchemist who placed it. Its wait lasts eight combat ticks.

Settled Batch keeps the vial's preparation potency. Split Pour still makes two smaller physical landings and spends one vial and one Standard Batch charge. Second Extraction pays its longer recovery and refunds an ingredient after the first actual heal from the throw group. An owned Flare can still be extinguished at impact. Use ordinary Cast Flask to open another Flash Reaction window or a Throw Latch. Pause freezes the field and wait. Losing the owner or leaving combat clears that owner's remaining work.
