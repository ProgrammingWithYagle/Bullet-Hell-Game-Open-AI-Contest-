**Rescue Run.** Use Action 2 while walking into a clear step. It spends the current Cleansing Serum recovery and gives up to 17 combat intervals of steerable movement at 145% usual walking speed. The Medic keeps ordinary body collisions, slows and exposure to damage. Other combat actions or health loss during the run end it. After 48 pixels of voluntary walking, stop and use Action 2 within 0.8 seconds for one 16-base focused dose. Aim at a wounded ally within 64 pixels along a clear line, or aim away from allies to treat yourself.

**Switchback.** After 36 pixels, use Action 2 during a turn of at least 60 degrees. A successful step earns seven extra combat intervals once. The original serum recovery and dose remain in effect.

**Moving Handoff.** After 48 pixels, aim and use Action 2 during another clear walking step. This delivers one 12-base dose and ends the run. The Medic can still choose to stop and give the 16-base dose. The two fittings exclude each other.

Hold Revive after reaching a downed teammate. A wall now blocks manual revival for every class, and the original difficulty restrictions and channel duration apply. The focused serum treats one living patient. Cleansing Serum retains the ordinary area heal and clears poison and slow. Both HUD layouts and the shop reflect owned choices and the saved remaps.
