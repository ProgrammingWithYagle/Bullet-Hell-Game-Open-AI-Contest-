# Ranger Tracker

Buy Dry Footing and Read the Sign with three earned Free Play skill points. After an ordinary primary arrow damages a visible living enemy, get near the place it hit and press Action 2. The latest observation appears as a ring. Up to three recent points from the same trail stay visible to the Ranger while the enemy remains in sight.

Read the Sign lasts 130 combat ticks. Walk across the line of the original shot and use an ordinary arrow to hit the same enemy again. The server checks where the follow-up arrow was actually released. A hit from at least 82 units to either side recovers one arrow up to the current quiver capacity. A second hit from the old lane spends an arrow as usual. The 278-tick recovery begins when signing the trail.

After five earned points, choose one fitting.

- **Trail Stakes** keeps the lateral route and lets a new Tripline finish the trail. The Ranger must lay it after activating Read the Sign. The actual cable must arm and damage the same enemy.
- **Backtrack** requires a different route. Move at least 100 units from the original firing point. Return to within 32 units and fire the follow-up from there. If the first move already took you far enough when you read the trail, you can return immediately. A shot already in the air before leaving cannot finish the trail.

The initial arrow hit expires after 125 combat ticks. Reading requires coming within 420 units of the observed contact. New samples require line of sight and a target within 510 units. Cover stops new samples. Drawn Line and Sight Draw use their own issued rules. Survey still modifies ordinary arrows through its normal charges. Tracker gives no damage bonus, redirect or extra attack. An interrupted or expired trail pays no refund.

The shop shows the available keyboard and controller remaps in both layouts. The owner receives the trail and the firing guide. Joined players see normal arrows and Triplines through the shared combat view.
