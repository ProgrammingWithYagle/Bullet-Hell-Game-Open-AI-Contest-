# Equipment and resource artwork

Three transparent painted icons use familiar objects: a leather equipment case, brass coins and an open field manual for skills. Shop tabs, resource totals and the unselected detail panel share them with the player coin/skill-point HUD. Written labels remain the primary explanation. Item-specific artwork and class symbols retain their existing uses.

The PNGs in [assets/production/ui/resources](../assets/production/ui/resources) are exact copies from the built-in image generator, with no image editing or resampling. [Prompts, original paths and hashes](../assets/production/ui/resources/prompts-v1.json) record the generation. All three are 1254×1254 RGBA with real transparent pixels and clear corners. CSS contains the complete square at 32px in the shop, 24px in the HUD and 80px in empty details (48px on compact layouts). Native inspection shows complete practical objects, warm worn metal/leather and clean silhouettes. Rendered menu-size/human/device review remains open.

Human feedback at testing5258 accepted the prior equipment navigation in a limited trial and requested a distinct equipment image. That feedback does not accept these new assets. Earlier art and receipts are preserved.
