# Grenadier implementation decisions

This Soldier discipline adds fuse timing and physical floor handling. Skirmisher retains vulnerable movement/loading; Marksman retains deliberate bank angles and earned follow-ups. Sniper, Foot Soldier and Hunter remain proposals whose distinctions still need work. This is not a fixed three-route ceiling or a whole-class completion claim.

Cooked Canister uses two C presses, with a finite running fuse. Preparation takes one shared recovery; a failed release preserves the remaining hand canister. Expiring in hand vents rather than introducing self-injury/healing farming or friendly fire. There is no ammunition purchase or new collectible stock: this is a finite kit ability, with at most one owned canister at a time. Ordinary Q/E/ultimate retain their effects. A held canister is a hand commitment; firing or incompatible actions cancel it, while thrown flight survives ordinary health loss.

The early casing fork changes contact behavior: one real skimming bank versus immediate impact burst. Underhand Cast adds a distinct short throw. Optional A- alternatives either store one exact remaining fuse through a bounded re-pin, freeing the hand, or retain the live fuse through the issued burst. They cost3SP versus2SP and are exclusive; neither is required for late ground handling. Toe Roll requires physically approaching a grounded canister; Live Return recovers that same object once without resetting its fuse, casing, kick, re-pin or recovery. Actual movement, cover and time constrain the combination. No target assistance or extra permanent input is added.

The foundation, one casing, Underhand, Re-pin, Toe Roll and Live Return cost20SP; Clipped Sling costs19SP; omitting the A- choice leaves additional General room.27SP/onecapstone remains. Acquired operations must be useful before the capstone. New techniques are actual behaviors, not empty rank cards or numerical-only purchases. The roster remains open beyond these eight techniques and beyond this third Soldier discipline.

Authority owns fuse, motion, body/cover contacts, one physical bank with remaining tick travel, floor drag, actual blast origin/armor, finite capacity, pauses, cancellation and owner/host cleanup. Friendly flight/update/removal streams stay owner-only. Finite shared contact/blast feedback omits private remaining-handling rights. No unfinished hand/flight/recovery/proof is restored or promoted to online proof. Validation must include immediate/cooked/short casts, bank/contact variants, moving body and cover order, blast occlusion/armor, retained fuse on kick/catch/re-pin, pool rejection, interruption, death/disconnect/host cleanup, both HUD layouts/remaps, real ordinary Short3SP purchase paths, complete optimized public/testing workers and real peers. The full foundation/one-case/Underhand/Re-pin/Toe/Return build costs20SP; Clipped Sling costs19SP. Optional preparation can be omitted.27SP/onecapstone leaves real General choices. Entry is affordable in ordinary Short3SP through two actual purchases; Raid12SP buys foundation/entry/case/Underhand for8SP. Acquired short throws and re-pin/sling work before the capstone.

A held primary trigger stays blocked through preparation; releasing then pressing again cancels the unsecured hand. Ordinary R/dodge/channel/overdrive/actual health loss and E without Sling interrupt it. A shield absorption does not count as health loss. Thrown flight survives ordinary damage while the owner lives. Pool rejection spends nothing on fresh preparation and preserves a failed held release. Owner/weapon/required-technique loss removes unfinished work; rostered spent recovery stays. Host exit removes actual flight. Pause freezes fuse/recovery/motion and discards paused input.

Skimming's bank allowance belongs to each manually thrown flight. A kick preserves that flight's already spent bank allowance. Catch removes that flight; a new manual throw supplies its normal initial speed and one casing bank again, within the original finite fuse and one-catch limit. It never restores kick or re-pin rights. This permits deliberate re-aiming without creating another charge or an endless bounce/catch chain.

Each physical leg uses only its remaining tick and moving-body interval. Blast samples the actual contact time and explosion origin for armor; cover occludes damage and independent weak bodies do not permit a boss-core bypass. Friendly trajectory/update/removal streams stay owner-only; finite shared hand/contact/blast/vent feedback omits private handling rights. The owned guide uses C/B and actual remapped R context, with four rows at entry/five after Underhand. No new permanent action slot.
