# Grenadier implementation decisions

This Soldier discipline adds fuse timing and physical floor handling. Skirmisher retains vulnerable movement/loading; Marksman retains deliberate bank angles and earned follow-ups. Sniper, Foot Soldier and Hunter remain proposals whose distinctions still need work. This is not a fixed three-route ceiling or a whole-class completion claim.

Cooked Canister uses two C presses, with a finite running fuse. Preparation takes one shared recovery; a failed release preserves the remaining hand canister. Expiring in hand vents rather than introducing self-injury/healing farming or friendly fire. There is no ammunition purchase or new collectible stock: this is a finite kit ability, with at most one owned canister at a time. Ordinary Q/E/ultimate retain their effects. A held canister is a hand commitment; firing or incompatible actions cancel it, while thrown flight survives ordinary health loss.

The early casing fork changes contact behavior: one real skimming bank versus immediate impact burst. Underhand Cast adds a distinct short throw. Optional A- alternatives either store one exact remaining fuse through a bounded re-pin, freeing the hand, or retain the live fuse through the issued burst. They cost3SP versus2SP and are exclusive; neither is required for late ground handling. Toe Roll requires physically approaching a grounded canister; Live Return recovers that same object once without resetting its fuse, casing, kick, re-pin or recovery. Actual movement, cover and time constrain the combination. No target assistance or extra permanent input is added.

The foundation, one casing, Underhand, Re-pin, Toe Roll and Live Return cost20SP; Clipped Sling costs19SP; omitting the A- choice leaves additional General room.27SP/onecapstone remains. Acquired operations must be useful before the capstone. New techniques are actual behaviors, not empty rank cards or numerical-only purchases. The roster remains open beyond these eight techniques and beyond this third Soldier discipline.

Authority owns fuse, motion, body/cover contacts, one physical bank with remaining tick travel, floor drag, actual blast origin/armor, finite capacity, pauses, cancellation and owner/host cleanup. Friendly flight/update/removal streams stay owner-only. Finite shared contact/blast feedback omits private remaining-handling rights. No unfinished hand/flight/recovery/proof is restored or promoted to online proof. Validation must include immediate/cooked/short casts, bank/contact variants, moving body and cover order, blast occlusion/armor, retained fuse on kick/catch/re-pin, pool rejection, interruption, death/disconnect/host cleanup, both HUD layouts/remaps, real ordinary Short3SP purchase paths, complete optimized public/testing workers and real peers. Natural outcomes and safe replay counterparts retain losses and all measured fields. Native checks are separate from human fun, rendered/device/language/listening/motion and final balance acceptance. All prior roots, private SQLite, content, ownership and Service Yard WIP remain preserved. Bosses/shop/Campaign stay later.
