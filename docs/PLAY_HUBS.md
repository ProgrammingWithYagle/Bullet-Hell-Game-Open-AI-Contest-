# Story Campaign and Free Play

Story Campaign and Free Play are separate home entries. Story opens an arc/chapter hub and then class, difficulty and review. Its canonical route always begins with Constable at Gatehouse. It does not appear in Free Play's route chooser. Free Play retains five routes and lets players choose class, map policy, difficulty and an optional ordinary opening boss, form and numeric seed in both testing and public audiences.

Custom boss/form/seed runs retain earned SP and income budgets. They carry a distinct customized flag, persist supported settings through legal boundary saves and do not award mastery, personal journal goals or team rankings. This uses the normal validation and authority path; testing-only injected money/power remains separate. Story rejects Free Play customization. Automatic ordinary routes keep their existing earned records. A forced difficulty form still takes priority over an opening-form choice.

Join Room has separate Story and Free Play tabs. Discovery can filter by room kind; code lookup identifies the kind, and an explicit mismatched join is rejected. A code for the other category asks the player to switch tabs. Categories share the existing host and capacity; no separate paid server or larger capacity is implied. Existing passwords, host ownership, pause, departure and reconnect policies remain. Protocol47 carries the new configuration and safe Story reading phase.

General abilities have their own shop tab beside equipment and the class skill tree. Sprint and Double Dash retain their existing prices, purchase guard and earned budget. Class-tree Classic/expanded view settings remain independent; inspecting or switching tabs does not buy anything.

The first arc, The Open Ledger, currently contains three chapters with five encounter scenes each, an expanded prologue, chapter conversations and ending epilogues. Existing painted locations and portraits frame a visual-novel reader with previous/next controls and an optional transcript. English, Spanish and Arabic each include fifty extra dialogue turns. Completed encounter scenes can be reread from the chapter archive. The reader changes only a bounded local cursor; it does not vote, launch combat, grant progress or write a profile. Character names remain preserved, and each prose block has explicit language/direction.

Story now enters an untimed saved storyScene after the retained five-second boss fall. Every connected eligible participant must press Ready before mutation selection and preparation begin. No combat ticks advance during reading. A disconnected participant does not block the remaining party; the normal explicit host pause still applies. Free Play retains its timers. Final ending results remain readable without a timer. Existing issued local saves migrate; online restoration stays exact. Old protocol46 Story saves do not acquire completed scenes or choices they did not earn.

This is the first implemented arc/chapters/scene framework, not the whole planned narrative. Larger later arcs, additional chapters, character expressions and new story artwork, twelve full narrative language editions, native-speaker/rendered/device review, human pacing and full-game acceptance remain unfinished. No voice acting or independent human playtest is claimed.
