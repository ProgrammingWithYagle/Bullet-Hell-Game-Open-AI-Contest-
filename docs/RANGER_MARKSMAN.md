# Ranger Marksman

Buy Set the Nock for 1 SP and Sight Draw for 2 SP at 3 earned SP. Aim and press Action 1 to reserve one ready arrow. Wait 18 combat ticks for the draw to settle, then press Action 1 within the 36-degree aim guides to release a real arrow from your current position.

You can walk at 66% of ordinary speed while holding the draw. Firing a primary arrow, replenishing or using other bow abilities waits until the hold ends. The draw stays open for at most 75 ticks from the first press. A sprint, ordinary dodge or expiration loses the reserved arrow. Recovery lasts 228 ticks from the first press.

Sight Draw fires one arrow with 76 base damage, 820 travel speed and 760 range. It stops on ordinary terrain and enemy contacts. The character's ability potency applies through the existing damage authority. Survey marks retain their charges for primary draws. Drawn Line and Crosswind keep their own arrow costs, damage and timing. Sight Draw takes a separate action slot.

## Fittings

- **Rolling Nock** carries a fully drawn arrow through one ordinary dodge. Release after landing within the original aim guides and deadline.
- **Change Bearing** lets you pick a new bearing once. Aim outside the guides and press Action 1, then wait 12 ticks for the new draw to settle. The turn keeps the reserved arrow and the original deadline.

Each fitting costs 2 SP at 5 earned SP and excludes the other. The local hotbar shows commitment, readiness and the new bearing prompt. Both players see the drawn arc. Recovery, fitting state and release readiness belong to the Ranger. A phase change, downing or disconnect drops the held arrow.
