# Bard Lament

Buy Clean Reed for 1 SP and Mourning Measure for 2 SP. Earn two cadence and press Action 2 while facing a clear lane. The measure spends Cut In recovery and briefly pauses primary fire. Stay near the start and face down the lane to sustain up to five pulses. Press again to end it early or use another ability to leave the lane.

Pulses use the current enemy armor and cover rules. Ordinary minions slow briefly on contact with the lane. Nearby masonry can close the lane. Teammates see the fixed line and its pulses. The Bard sees the preview and current posture.

- **Cross Verse.** After the first pulse, aim toward an adjacent clear lane and press Action 2 to turn once. The turn costs a pulse and keeps the original start and deadline.
- **Closing Chord.** After two damaging pulses, face the lane and press Action 2 to release one chord at the far end. The chord spends the remaining pulses.

Either fitting costs 2 SP at 5 earned SP. Choose one over the course of a run.
