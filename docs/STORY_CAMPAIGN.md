# The Open Ledger

The sixth mode is a continuous fifteen-encounter story campaign for one to eight players. Three acts follow the evacuation ledger through Lorn. Original prose is authored in content/source/the-open-ledger.json; the deterministic route helper is src/shared/story-campaign.ts. The canonical updater is tools/update-story-campaign.mjs, also included in the full content builder.

After each of the first fourteen fights, the normal mutation vote is followed by a 45-second party route vote. Shelter, Witness and Refusal cards pair an authored next location with a concealed fixed-grade contract. Normal vote ownership and seeded ties apply. Choices commit once at resolution; the next fight uses that choice's arena. Boss identities remain concealed until deployment. The opening is always Constable in Gatehouse. Sixty-two ordinary candidates are available before the finale; City Eater, Last Breath Saint and Last Knocker are reserved for the final vote. No boss repeats within a run. Existing fixed grades and forced advanced difficulty forms apply.

The earned skill budget rises from 3 to 27SP across fifteen deployments; the income curve uses the existing 120–2400 base budget and difficulty multiplier. General purchases share that budget. The mode grants no extra skill points, persistent combat power or free rewards for reading.

The most chosen stance selects one of three endings, with the latest tied stance breaking a tie. A personal full-run receipt must include deployment from the first encounter, fifteen credited campaign clears and fourteen committed choices. Profile displays recovered endings for rereading. A run/player receipt contributes at most one ending: the first banked choice history survives old checkpoint views or branch rewinds. Late joins receive their actual participation credit, and modified testing runs receive no personal replay credit. The completed story is banked before the optional Uncounted extension and survives extension failure.

Story screens live in setup, the existing intermission overlay and results. They do not add combat overlays, automatic multiplayer pause or a new timer outside existing phase ownership. Details can be closed; their state survives same-phase menu refresh. The ordinary host pause controls remain available. Offline and authoritative boundaries carry bounded route history; protocol 46 is required online. Known issued protocol 45 local saves migrate only for modes that existed then. Profiles keep schemaVersion 1 with a conditional Story receipt field; forged old-revision Story records are rejected.

The complete narrative is currently original English, explicitly marked lang=en and dir=ltr even inside right-to-left menus. Story interface labels are authored in all fifteen catalogs. Fourteen full narrative translations, native-language review, rendered/device review, human pacing and balance, voices, companion bots and full-game acceptance remain unfinished. No actor voice recordings or new character animations are claimed. Headless progression and native DOM controller checks are software evidence, not human playtesting.
