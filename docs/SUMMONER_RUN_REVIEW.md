## Standard comparisons

Each pass paired all eight possible combinations of the three fitting choices in three seeded Solo and two-player openings. The paired runs used the same purchase sequence through the clears both reached. Twelve cases from each pass replayed exactly. [Raw runs and replays](../reviews/summoner-whole-review-2026-11-24/whole-summoner-receipts.zip).

The first pass used the ordinary policy in Gatehouse on Normal and in Sunken Chapel and Ossuary Works on Baby, with arena routing enabled. Across its 96 runs no team earned every fitting or completed Standard. Gatehouse and Sunken Chapel reached as far as their third fight, which was against Bellringer or Glass Surgeon. Ossuary Works stalled at the opening Constable fight. [First pass](../reviews/summoner-whole-review-2026-11-24/baseline-summary.json).

The tactical pass used fixed arenas on Baby. The policy steered around visible beam lines and aimed at reachable lenses and nearby ordinary enemies. Every row includes eight Ossuary Works starts that still stopped in the opening fight. The other two arenas reached the fourth-clear shop in all 64 cases. In the active rows, an assigned attack records an issued companion attack during a Cohort march, a repair records a real Mending Bond heal, and a catch records a hostile shot intercepted by Formation Keeper.

## Practice and controlled combinations

All 16 active Solo runs that reached the fourth-clear shop restored from their actual Practice checkpoint at 13 earned SP. Each kept its seven purchased cards, ready ammunition and Q/E/ultimate clocks. All 16 reproduced their combat inputs, route, events and result after restoration. Fifteen finished Standard. [Restoration receipts](../reviews/summoner-whole-review-2026-11-24/tactical-summary.json).

One controlled fight paid for Set the Line, caught a shot, repaired the same stationed companion with Close Stitch and replanted through Quarter Turn before taking a second catch. After the line ended, a real damaging attack earned Relay. The exact ordered Lanternwing projectile then damaged that mark. In the two-Summoner case a departing owner's line cleared, the other owner's Walking Rank retained its first catch across a replant, and Homeward Thread repaired the selected unit on arrival. Separate legal Running Pack, Closed Ranks and Patient Company builds each caught a real shot using permanent bodies while their two Wickerwards stayed out of the formation. [Controlled cases](../reviews/summoner-whole-review-2026-11-24/controlled.json).
