# Swordsman Counterstroke

Buy Sound Edge for 1 SP and Drawn Reply for 2 SP. Press Action 2 to spend Frontal Guard recovery. The blade can stop one incoming projectile during the next eleven combat ticks. A real stop opens 28 ticks for a manually aimed answer. Press Action 2 again to release the cut. Walking is allowed. A dodge, sprint, reload or another ability breaks the reply.

The game compares the guard with earlier contact against terrain, bodies and barriers. Holding the guard also holds primary fire. Frontal Guard on E keeps its three-shot guard and separate primary counter. One player can own both Measured Stance and Counterstroke and use them at different moments.

- **Needle Return.** Aim back along the captured shot’s travel and press Action 2. One finite point travels until cover or its first target.
- **Back Answer.** After the stop, either face the original heading for the usual reply or turn at least 45 degrees for a sweep beside or behind you. Each choice uses the one answer.

Each fitting costs 2 SP at 5 earned SP. Choose one per run.
