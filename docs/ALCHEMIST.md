# Alchemist / Perrin Moss

The twelfth shared discipline prepares finite recipes and places delayed flasks. Perrin recently entered Alchemist training after working in a dyehouse. Heda Linn is another practitioner, introduced in the English ward-porch scene. Neither person represents the whole discipline, and neither has a completed personal arc.

## Prepare, place, combine

Your primary bellows fires real dosing projectiles. It has its own samples and refill clock. Ingredients are a separate combat stock: start each encounter with three cinder, three chalk and three sap, with a maximum of six each. An actual positive primary hit can restore one least-stocked ingredient, at most once per second. A separate active-combat clock restores one ingredient every five seconds. Empty shots restore nothing. This tactical stock does not grant Campaign money or persistent materials.

Use the recipe buttons, or the rebindable class command, to select a recipe. Q, Small Batch, immediately spends its ingredients and takes 0.4 seconds to prepare a bottle. Move at 80% speed during that commitment; primary fire is unavailable. A dodge cancels preparation without a refund. Keep up to two completed bottles. Changing the selected recipe does not change bottles already prepared.

| Recipe | Cost | Initial effect | Ground effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flare | 1 cinder + 1 chalk | 55 damage, radius 58 | Eight-second pool: five pulses of 4 damage, spaced 1.5 seconds apart |
| Binding | 1 chalk + 1 sap | 25 damage, radius 64; ordinary enemies slowed by 40% for three seconds | Eight-second pool with five brief ordinary-only slow refreshes |
| Mending | 1 cinder + 1 sap | Restore 18 actual missing health, radius 76 | No damage or persistent pool |

E, Cast Flask, throws the oldest prepared bottle to a chosen point up to 300 world units away. Mouse placement follows the pointer. Touch and gamepad placement follows stick distance, including a neutral self placement. The accepted landing stays fixed through later movement or turns. Flight lasts 0.5 seconds. Cover blocks placement and splash; a later obstruction can make the landing fail without refunding the bottle. A deliberately close or self throw remains possible. Base Q cooldown is four seconds; base E cooldown is six.

Land your Flare inside your own Binding pool to consume that pool and add 30 base impact damage. Land Mending inside your own Flare to extinguish it, giving up its remaining pulses without adding healing. These combinations work at the ordinary six-second throw cooldown. They cannot consume someone else's pools or erase hostile warnings. Bosses take damage but are not rooted and keep their attack commitments.

R, Standard Batch, restores one of each ingredient within its cap. For fourteen seconds, your next three actual throws gain 50% potency and 25% radius. Prepare bottles and have E available before beginning if you want all three throws at the base cooldown. A miss spends a charge. Activating R alone deals no damage and grants no invulnerability. Base cooldown is 95 seconds.

Reagents, Mixtures and Dyehouse contain 54 nodes across nine paths. Four dosing weapons and the usual equipment upgrades affect actual primary attacks. The sample case uses the existing authoritative ammo-pouch purchase: it refills primary samples, without restoring ingredients, prepared bottles, ability clocks or batch charges.

| Transformation | Benefit | Cost or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Split Pour | Two real landings, offset 24 units to each side | Each has 55% potency and 72% radius; cover can block one. The pair spends one bottle and at most one batch charge. |

Per owner, at most six flights and four pools exist. New pools retire the oldest owned ground at capacity. Leaving combat, losing an active owner or disconnecting clears their preparation, flights, ground and owned primary shots. All damage and healing credit comes from actual useful effects.
