# Tank Interceptor

Interception Line lets Tank spend the same recovery as Rescue Charge on a brief directional screen. Aim, press Action 3 to prepare, release, and press again to commit. Preparation takes six combat intervals, and leaving it unused for ninety intervals spends no charge. The screen lasts ten intervals. Forward travel covers 180 pixels on clear ground. A third press brakes without refunding recovery.

| Purchase | First ordinary Short opportunity | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interception Line | 2 SP after Riveted Coat at the 3 SP start | Screen a narrow front while advancing along the captured bearing. |
| Crossing Guard | 2 SP after entry at 9 earned SP | Give Action 3 a sideways movement direction to cross the front while facing the threat. |
| Standfast | Alternative 2 SP after entry at 9 earned SP | Hold the same directional screen in place. Walking or Action 3 releases it. |
