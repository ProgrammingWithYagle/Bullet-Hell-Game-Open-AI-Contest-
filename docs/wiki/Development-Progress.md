## Latest human feedback

- Constable became slightly harder; being one of the easiest bosses is appropriate. Do not turn its Normal form into a high-threat encounter.
- Phase transitions still felt subtle, and Brutal looked and played too similarly. The latest phase-cue, red treatment and extra-sequence implementation awaits another human check.
- Music was acceptable but could be a little more intense. A quiet tempo-aligned percussion layer now responds to phase and Brutal; human listening review remains open.
- Earlier map bullets and warnings were readable on Normal with Hollow Constable. This is limited evidence, not full map/device acceptance.

## Count definitions

Boss quality counts a completed implementation of the requested phase/mobility/Brutal pass, followed by separate human review. The other 59 existing encounters await assessment or redesign; they are already playable. Class revamps count the newly requested ability-changing upgrade pass, separate from the seven existing class kits and 378 skills. Map layout counts the five requested secondary-map topology revisions; their implementation is complete, while detailed new-layout human acceptance remains pending.

The three new Ashen Usher, Brass Toad and Ragpicker's Kite references are preserved production concepts, not new encounters. The structured ledger, derived counts, in-game journal summary and this page update together. Progress does not equal final balance, rendered, device, language or release acceptance.
