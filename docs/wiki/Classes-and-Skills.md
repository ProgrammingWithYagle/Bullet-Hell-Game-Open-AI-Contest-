# Classes and skills

| Class | Survivor | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Soldier | Mara Rook | Reliable ranged damage and squad leadership. |
| Heavy | Hux Bell | Sustained fire and suppression. |
| Tank | Bram Voss | Protection, interruption and rescue. |
| Medic | Iona Vale | Healing, cleansing and rapid recovery. |
| Mage | Sable Quill | Area control and magical burst damage. |
| Engineer | Tamsin Reed | Deployables, repair and controlled ground. |
| Assassin | Vale Ash | Precision, mobility and safe rescue openings. |

Every class has 18 nodes across three branches. Campaigns reach 27 SP before the final boss; a full branch costs 21. Purchased power resets between runs. Mastery and cosmetics persist. All seven classes are available immediately.
