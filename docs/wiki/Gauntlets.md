# Gauntlets and boss selection

The authored roster contains 60 identities, separated into 50 timed-route bosses, nine Endless-exclusive bosses and The Uncounted. **50 are currently executable**: nine in Quarantine, fourteen in Midnight, twenty in Pilgrimage and seven in Raid. The other 10 require implementation.

| Pool | Authored identities | Playable now | Route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarantine | 9: six regular, three finale | All 9 | Four regulars, then one finale |
| Midnight | 14: eleven regular, three finale | All 14 | Nine regulars, then one finale |
| Pilgrimage | 20: seventeen regular, three finale | All 20 | Fifteen regulars, then one finale |
| Raid | 7: four regular, three finale | All 7 | Two regulars, then one finale |
| Endless exclusives | 9 | 0 | Future special schedule |
| The Uncounted | 1 | 0 | Future optional extension |

Normal Quarantine, Midnight, Pilgrimage and Raid progression never repeat an identity within a run. Votes expose only the current candidates; later bosses stay concealed. Finales are reserved for the last encounter. A manually selected opening boss is a rehearsal override and can intentionally bypass that ordinary opening pool.

Current Endless draws all 50 implemented identities before repeated identities become Brutal and avoids immediate identical repeats. Its full authored twelve-encounter cooldown and guaranteed exclusive schedule still require the remaining content and runtime work. Endless has no artificial ending or unlimited purchased player power.
