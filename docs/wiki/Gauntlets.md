# Expanded boss production plan - edition 0.2.0

Current local feature-branch source: 23 playable bosses and individual themes, seven classes, six different map footprints, Quarantine and the ten-fight Midnight Circuit, protocol 9. The remaining 37 identities, later routes and full release/visual/device acceptance are unfinished. Older checkpoint counts below are historical. The public Site remains its earlier protocol-4 deployment; source/Wiki pushes do not redeploy it.


The revised target is **60 distinct bosses**, including The Uncounted. The original eleven images are an early reference proof, not the final roster. A boss counts toward sixty only when its identity, silhouette, signature mechanic, counterplay, phases, audio warning and acceptance evidence are implemented. A recolor, threat multiplier or repeated champion phase does not count as a new boss.

The target is sixty because it supports route variety while leaving enough production and testing attention for each encounter. One hundred would add a substantial animation, translation, balance and playtesting burden. Additional bosses can follow in dated updates after the first complete release; the changelog must distinguish delivered content from a target.

The four route pools are disjoint. Their counts ensure there are still three unused regular candidates at the last regular vote, plus three reserved finales for the final vote. Players see threat, bounty and build recommendations; identities remain concealed. All fifty route bosses have threat-scaled versions, with complete opening tells and difficulty-bounded complexity. Mode duration and progression budgets stay unchanged.

The six arena kits are reusable places within these routes. A map theme and a route's encounter pool are separate concepts: each timed experience has its own gauntlet even when its path visits the same district. Boss-specific props and hazard layouts vary within collision-safe arena boundaries.

## Quarantine Run - the broken cordon

Regulars: Hollow Constable, Carrion Courier, The Tollkeeper, Lantern Warden, Ratcatcher, Quarantine Clerk.

Finales: Chain Marshal, Gate Widow, Black Ambulance.

This route teaches aimed volleys, locked target positions, scent trails, reflected wheel lanes and temporary gate openings. The finale resolves the evacuation cordon rather than reusing the Pilgrimage's council ending.

## Midnight Circuit - services of the dead

Regulars: Cradle Matron, Mold Alchemist, Bellringer, Stitchwright, Glass Surgeon, Ash Baker, Sluice Keeper, Last Stationmaster, The Lamplighter, The Anatomist, Plague Gardener.

Finales: Grave Choir, Red Abbess, Vesper Judge.

This route uses hospital summons, safe ground, timed gaps, rescue tethers, broken polygons, split projectiles, controlled currents and alternating verdicts. Its ending restores the infirmary and chapel network.

## Black Bell Pilgrimage - industry and memory

Regulars: Pallbearer, Cinder Foreman, Canal Diver, Clockmender, Seamstress of Wires, Gravedigger, Ink Notary, Spore Beekeeper, Ice Custodian, Bridge Ferryman, Porcelain Mourner, Boiler Tender, Mirror Undertaker, Chalk Magistrate, Wire Harpist, Crow Shepherd, Ledger Leech.

Finales: Mortuary Engine, Drowned Regent, Salt Patriarch.

This route has enough identities for sixteen fights without repeating one. It explores moving cover, bounded terrain motion, readable replays, removable anchors, pressure valves and guarded weak points. Its ending severs the council command.

## Grand Raid - city-scale horrors

Regulars: Chain Colossus, Ossuary Leviathan, Bell Triplets, Quarantine Heart.

Finales: Crown Procession, City Eater, Saint of the Last Breath.

Raid bosses use longer authored encounters and larger shared opportunities. Objectives scale down to one player and never require a particular class. Weak points and neutral valves create cooperation without making damage depend on simultaneous specialized roles.

## Endless exclusives - the city after midnight

Hourglass Widow, Unlit Astronomer, Folded Man, Red Comet, Memory Taxer, Choir of Moths, Zero-Hour Conductor, Walking District, The Last Knocker.

These introduce delayed path replays, orbital gaps, reflected bullet directions, ordered dash trails, accessible symbol history, curved ribbons, beat-marked lanes, moving building blocks and a counted door sweep. They do not rewind player inputs, invent invisible hazards or require sound, color perception or unaided memorization.

From encounter four, every fourth encounter offers three exclusive boss candidates, guaranteeing an exclusive fight regardless of vote. The other encounters draw from the fifty route identities. The first three encounters use a readable opening subset. Keep a twelve-encounter identity cooldown; prefer unseen bag entries before reshuffling eligible identities. The Uncounted retains the existing optional campaign unlock and is not a surprise random Endless spawn.

## Clean detail art standard

Use three readable value groups, one dominant silhouette, and one signature prop. Concentrate fine detail on the face, hands and prop. Most clothing panels and body masses have broad matte color fields. Avoid all-over crackle, dense filigree, dozens of tiny hanging objects, dust clouds and glowing outlines. Internal lines must merge gracefully at gameplay size rather than creating speckled noise.

Review survivor art at 56 world pixels and boss art at 150, on both quiet arena ground and a crowded hostile pattern. At those sizes, a player must recognize the class or boss and its attack pose. Hostile projectiles retain clear high-contrast shapes and hitboxes; decorative detail never competes with them. High-detail source art alone is insufficient evidence.

Treat the original proof set as identity/palette reference. Generate cleaner finish references before new animation frames. Do not repaint or simplify images with ad hoc procedural drawing; generated revisions preserve original tool files, prompts and review decisions. Atlas slicing, common-scale resampling and transparent padding remain deterministic asset processing.

## Gates and evidence

Edition 0.1.0 remains a sealed historical packet. Edition 0.2.0 must regenerate the full catalog, route selection, analytical balance, all fifteen boss catalogs, asset manifest, illustrated bible and portable verification. Existing Stage 1 evidence does not establish that the expanded runtime is complete.

The first gameplay milestone remains Soldier and Medic, Gatehouse and Hollow Constable, both votes, shopping, revives, offline/public/private play and the active fifty-player feasibility test. Produce the full sixty-boss art/animation library after that architecture passes. Reusable simulation primitives are encouraged; unique signature behaviors and actual tests establish a distinct boss.

Every shipped boss needs: three-phase combat smoke test, signature/counterplay check, solo and party objective check, deadline/uptime measurement, all fifteen launch locales complete, source-art identity and motion review, and matching codex/release notes. The numerical target does not justify recording unimplemented content as released.
