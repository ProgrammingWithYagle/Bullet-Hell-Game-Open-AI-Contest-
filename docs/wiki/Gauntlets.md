# Gauntlets and boss selection

The authored roster contains 60 identities, separated into 50 timed-route bosses, nine Endless-exclusive bosses and The Uncounted. **All 60 execute**: fifty in timed-route pools, nine Endless-exclusive identities and the optional Uncounted finale. Full rendered/action, deadline, balance, human and device acceptance remain separate unfinished gates.

| Pool | Authored identities | Playable now | Route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quarantine | 9: six regular, three finale | All 9 | Four regulars, then one finale |
| Midnight | 14: eleven regular, three finale | All 14 | Nine regulars, then one finale |
| Pilgrimage | 20: seventeen regular, three finale | All 20 | Fifteen regulars, then one finale |
| Raid | 7: four regular, three finale | All 7 | Two regulars, then one finale |
| Endless exclusives | 9 | All 9 | Every fourth Endless encounter, starting with fight four |
| The Uncounted | 1 | Executable | Optional qualified campaign extension |

Normal Quarantine, Midnight, Pilgrimage and Raid progression never repeat an identity within a run. Votes expose only the current candidates; later bosses stay concealed. Finales are reserved for the last encounter. A manually selected opening boss is a rehearsal override and can intentionally bypass that ordinary opening pool.

Endless uses seeded bags for fifty route identities and nine exclusives. Every fourth fight offers three exclusives, so every vote result is exclusive. The first three fights use readable opening candidates. A twelve-completed-encounter cooldown prevents repeats; unchosen candidates remain in the bag. Previously defeated identities return as Brutal. Selection history and seeded choices survive boundary saves. The Uncounted is excluded from random selection. Endless has no artificial ending or unlimited purchased player power.
