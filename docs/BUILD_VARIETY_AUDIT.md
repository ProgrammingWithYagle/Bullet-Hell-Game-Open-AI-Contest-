# Scoped build variety audit

Actual canonical purchases verified **2,262 distinct builds** under the 27-SP cap across Engineer, Soldier and Heavy specializations and four shared techniques. All 2,226 prior builds remain legal. Reserve Anchor adds 36 Heavy builds, with 12 each for bare hauling, Straight Yoke and Open Sheave. The count excludes legacy numerical variants and foundation-only duplicates. The 48 seeded Short trials span all six arenas, Baby and Normal, two seeds and two matched hauling policies. The direct-anchor policy recorded 17 victories and 7 defeats.
