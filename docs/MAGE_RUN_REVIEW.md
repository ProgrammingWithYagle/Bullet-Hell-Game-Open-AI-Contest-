## Mixed operations

In a controlled 16-earned-point fight, Sable bought the three entries, Hold the Trace and Twin Vent for 13 points. A paid Pivot Sigil and an actual walked Waymark Return charged the seal. The Mage held the return in place, hit one target for 170.1, took a 14 HP projectile wound and turned for a second hit worth 182.7. The wound shortened the fuse. Both releases fit within the hold. The remainder of the return used its first E clock.

A second 16-earned-point build used Shunt Fuse and Side Landing. Binding Sigil took its own Q while the unpaid pivot cleared. Ward Detonation took its own E while the unpaid return cleared and charged the seal. Sable shifted the paid Ward charge while walking, then released on the same Silent Twelfth recovery.
