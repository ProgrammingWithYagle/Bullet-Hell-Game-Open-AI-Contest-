# Sniper: first working operation

This is the next Soldier slice after the verified Grenadier checkpoint. It does not set a route maximum, finish Soldier, or register promised later nodes. Existing Skirmisher, Marksman and Grenadier behavior stays intact.

The first operation is **Settled Shot**. Action 1 prepares one actual magazine round and starts recovery. The Soldier must remain at the planted position while the sight settles, then deliberately press action 1 again to fire. There is no automatic shot at the end of a timer. Aim can move within the settled twelve-degree arc; moving beyond it recenters the sight and starts settling again, within the original finite preparation lifetime. The shot follows the player's actual final manual aim. It is a direct physical round: bodies, cover, travel, armor and time still matter.

Implemented first-operation values for measured iteration: D-, 2SP after 1SP Clean Bore, affordable with ordinary Short's 3SP. One actual round is reserved on preparation; ten-second base recovery, 0.7-second settling, three-second total preparation, 110 base ability damage, 1,400px/s, radius3, 1.2-second flight. No piercing, bank, target selection, armor bypass or extra collected resource. These values support the operation; they are not the path's identity or a final balance verdict.

Movement cancels preparation instead of rooting the player or granting protection. Actual health loss, a fresh primary press after release, Reload, dodge, channel, burst and overdrive also cancel it. A held primary trigger is blocked through preparation. Interruption/expiry loses the reserved round and keeps recovery; an empty magazine, busy body or full projectile pool rejects fresh preparation without spending. A rejected release retains its one paid round and original deadline. Owner/weapon/technique loss removes unfinished preparation and actual flight, with rostered recovery retained. No mid-combat preparation, flight or recovery becomes a saved proof. Friendly flight/removal stays owner-only; finite shared sight/contact feedback carries no hidden payment state.

This differs from Ranger's issued bow. Ranger commits aim for six ticks on primary or twelve on Drawn Line, moves at its issued reduced speed, then releases automatically from the later owner position. Settled Shot requires a planted body, a bounded sight that can be deliberately recentered, and a separate manual release. It does not sell Ranger's moving draw, tripline, Survey charges or a stronger renamed bow attack. Marksman controls physical cover angles and contact-earned reload/relay opportunities; Sniper prepares a direct line and chooses when to spend a finite stationary opportunity. Grenadier manipulates a running fuse and a floor object; Skirmisher commits vulnerable movement/loading. No old operation is purchased again.

The meaningful early decisions are when it is safe to plant, whether to hold an already settled sight for an opening, whether to recenter and sacrifice time, and whether to abandon the paid round to escape. Shared Rally/Precision Burst/Last Roll Call keep their own effects. Later internal forks and acquired operations must grow from observed play; possible names and quantity remain unregistered until their mechanics earn a distinction. The first operation must work before any later capstone exists.
