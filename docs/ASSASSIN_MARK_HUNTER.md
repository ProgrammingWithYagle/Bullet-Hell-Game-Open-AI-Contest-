# Assassin Mark Hunter

Buy Light Soles for 1 SP and Crossing Writ for 2 SP. Aim at a visible enemy and press Action 3 to mark it for up to 4.8 seconds. Land two separate primary hits on that same target. Aim the marked line across the enemy’s expected path and press again to place it on clear floor. The cutoff spends the original Unwritten Passage recovery and waits for up to 2.9 seconds. The quarry’s center must move across the fixed segment for the strike. The earlier path and ordinary abilities stay available in the same build.

A new obstruction closes the line. The formation must start ahead of the quarry’s center. The strike uses the encounter’s existing armor and weak-point rules. Teammates see the marked quarry and paid line. Proof, aim guides and action sounds are visible or audible to the owner.

- **Canted Cutoff.** Press after confirmation to pin a location. Turn the line around that point and press again before the original mark expires. The second press places the line and pays the recovery.
- **Transfer Writ.** After placing the cutoff, nominate another visible enemy and land one new primary hit before the line expires. The line keeps its position and deadline. One transfer is available per paid line.

Each fitting costs 2 SP and unlocks at 5 earned SP. They exclude each other.
