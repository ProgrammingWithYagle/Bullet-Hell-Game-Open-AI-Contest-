# Summoner Cohort Commander

Buy Call and Answer for 1 SP and Cohort Drive for 2 SP. Hit an enemy with your primary beacon and call two living permanent companions. Press Action 1 to spend one Focus and send the pair to separate approach points. Each walks through real terrain, attacks once from legal range and resumes its ordinary order.

- **Cross Orders** sends the pair around opposite sides of the mark. It suits enemies with exposed flanks and arenas with space for both approaches.
- **Relay Orders** sends a leader first. Its ordered attack must actually damage the mark. The resulting opening lasts 59 combat ticks. Press Action 1 again to send the same second companion toward your current beacon mark. The follow-up uses the Focus already spent.

Each fitting costs 2 SP at 5 earned SP. Buying one excludes the other. Gather and a new ordinary retinue order clear the current march. The owner gets the control prompts, while both views show the public march markers. A lost companion leaves its assignment empty.
