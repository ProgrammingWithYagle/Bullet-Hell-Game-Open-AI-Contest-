# Bard Conductor

Buy Leave a Breath for 1 SP and Call and Response for 2 SP. Earn two cadence with primary notes, aim at clear ground and press Action 1. The call spends Resolving Phrase recovery and the current cadence. A pulse sounds at your feet and marks a reply stage. Walk to that stage and land a primary note fired from it. Press Action 1 again before the mark closes to place the final pulse.

Each pulse damages enemies and shields allies in range under the existing difficulty rules. Nearby masonry can close a reply. Teammates see the shared stage and pulse positions. Ready prompts, aiming guides and the new action sounds reach the Bard who paid for the call. Q still performs the ordinary three-pulse phrase.

- **Side Stage.** Earn one fresh cadence before confirming the reply. Face a new site and press Action 1 to move the same mark. The reply still ends at its original deadline and needs a new note from the moved stage.
- **Reprise.** Once a note confirms the stage, turn back toward the original call before pressing Action 1. One finishing pulse returns to that ground. Other aim directions leave it at the reply stage.

Each fitting costs 2 SP and unlocks at 5 earned SP. Choose one for the run.
