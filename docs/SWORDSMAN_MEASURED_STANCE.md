# Swordsman Measured Stance

Buy Set the Distance for 1 SP and Held Edge for 2 SP. Stand still and press Action 1 to spend Measured Cut recovery. Hold your footing until the arc settles. Face along it and press again to cut without moving. Walking, dodging, sprinting, reloading or using another ability breaks the stance.

The hold takes 17 combat ticks and ends 57 ticks after planting. While it is held, primary fire waits and Long Measure keeps its primary-cut charges. The cut uses Dara’s ordinary blade collision, including cover, armor and one eligible boss part. The owner sees the next action. Teammates see the held and released arcs.

- **Turn the Edge.** Once ready, aim 20 to 80 degrees from the first heading and press again to turn once. The new arc takes nine ticks to settle and uses the same deadline.
- **Second Line.** After actual first damage, keep your footing and aim 20 to 95 degrees beside the original cut. Press again within 22 ticks for one smaller cut.

Either fitting costs 2 SP at 5 earned SP. Choose one per run.
