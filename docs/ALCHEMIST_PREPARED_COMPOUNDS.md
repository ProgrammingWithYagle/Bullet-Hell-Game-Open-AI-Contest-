# Alchemist Prepared Compounds

Buy A Measured Dose and Stow Flask with three earned Free Play skill points. Prepare a Flare, then a Binding. Press Action 2 to stow the front Flare. The stowed flask still takes one of your prepared slots, and the recipe panel shows its remaining time. You can now throw the Binding first with Cast Flask.

With the standard sleeve, a second Action 2 puts the Flare at the front after 1.2 seconds. The sleeve holds the flask for up to seven seconds. Once that time passes, the flask is lost and the slot opens. The returned flask keeps its original recipe and preparation potency. It still needs an ordinary throw opening or a qualifying Flash Reaction.

Two fittings become available at five earned points.

- **Brew Latch** opens after you finish brewing a different recipe. To throw the Binding first, stow the Flare, cast the Binding and complete a Mending brew before the sleeve expires. A cancelled brew leaves the latch closed.
- **Throw Latch** opens when Cast Flask actually launches a different recipe. Stow the Flare and throw the Binding to bring the Flare straight back to the front. A blocked throw leaves the latch closed. Flash Reaction also leaves it closed.

Settled Batch still has three real slots, including a sleeve in use. Split Pour spends one returned bottle and a single batch charge. Second Extraction still pays its longer throw recovery and refunds an ingredient only when the initial splash does useful work. The combat clock pauses with the match. Leaving combat or losing the owner clears the sleeve. Local Practice restores purchased ownership at safe boundaries.
