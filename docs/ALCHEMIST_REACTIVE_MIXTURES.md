# Alchemist Reactive Mixtures

Buy Keep Two Apart and Flash Reaction with three earned Free Play skill points. Prepare a Binding, then a Flare. Throw the Binding with Cast Flask. While Cast Flask is recovering, aim at the live pool and press Action 1. The owner’s guide shows the selected pool.

The advance spends the front Flare and one extra chalk. It adds one full ordinary throw recovery to the already scheduled ready time. With four seconds left on Cast Flask, for example, the next throw waits those four seconds plus one full recovery. Each ordinary Cast Flask allows one advance.

The Flask follows an actual fixed flight. A new obstruction can stop the paid throw before landing. The default reaction consumes the selected Binding at impact. Short Wick and Vent Cap are alternate fittings available at five earned points.

- **Short Wick** reaches a Binding within 190 units and lands in nine combat ticks. The default reaches 300 units and lands in 15.
- **Vent Cap** gives up the combination burst and keeps the chosen Binding until its natural expiry. If the pool limit forces a retirement, the oldest other pool gives way first.

Split Pour keeps its two physical landings and a single shared batch charge. Only the chosen Binding can fuel the early reaction. Settled Batch keeps its actual preparation time and potency. Second Extraction keeps its longer recovery and returns an ingredient when the initial splash delivers a real useful effect.
