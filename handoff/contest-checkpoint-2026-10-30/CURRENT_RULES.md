# Accepted rules to carry forward

Medic revives restore 100%health plus a nonstacking 20%maximum-HP shield on Baby, 75% on Easy, 45% on Normal, 30% on Hard and 1 HP on Insane. Hellish permits no revives. Other classes restore 100/50/25% on Baby/Easy/Normal and cannot revive on Hard or above. Medic can revive self or teammates; other classes self-revive only in Solo. The finite two-second channel consumes a charge on success, resets on release and grants one-second protection. One base charge per fight plus purchased charges remain.

Passive regeneration for all classes is reduced 25% on Hard and 50% on Insane, and disabled on Hellish. Active healing is separate. Personal shield caps are 100% of maximum HP on lower difficulties, 75% on Hard, 50% on Insane and zero on Hellish, floored to integers. Physical cover walls are separate. Tank starts at 100% of that cap, Heavy 50%, Soldier 15%, other classes zero. Future shield-cap class branches are deferred.

A specifically chosen map stays fixed throughout the run, including extra encounters. Random per run chooses once; random per encounter can repeat. The five-second Boss defeated moment keeps the current floor and uses available fall/final-pose artwork plus sound and preparation text. Fifteen bosses have full falls; fifty-one use final poses. Do not claim 66 full falling animations.

Online menus and chat do not pause the match. The host has explicit Pause match/Continue match buttons. Leaving transfers control and resumes play; ending for everyone is a separate confirmed action. Code and password are shown only to the host as authorized; password eye buttons are supported. Chat C toggles, Enter types, IME/drafts and server-confirmed deduplication remain.

Keep character headshots, independent health and shield bars, readable currencies and full menu space. Retain the accepted gallery, wider camera, upper map routes/Brass Toad detours, fixed corner actions and pit-only lava artwork. New player language choice and setup-bound automatic names remain. Plain words take priority over lore wording.
